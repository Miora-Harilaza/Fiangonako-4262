# FJKM ANTSOMAFI — design direction
Reference: corporate-1 screenshot supplied by the user. Warm full-bleed photographic hero inside a rounded frame, airy white canvas, acidic lime calls to action, forest-green type. Adapt to a welcoming church, not corporate consulting.
Typography: Poppins for body and headings; large restrained medium-weight headings, small tracked uppercase section labels. Color: #203a30 forest, #c0ef38 lime, #f5f6f1 ivory, #69736b muted; no purple. Desktop max-width 1320px, 28px outer gutter, 80px section spacing. Mobile stack and hamburger navigation. Hero: photo with dark left gradient, navigation above inside frame, bottom translucent information strip. Three editorial feature cards, highlighted lime middle card; event rows; pale-green mission block, photographic gallery, contact footer.
Logo: custom proposed cross inside a rounded lime square, wordmark FJKM / ANTSOMAFI. Not an official denominational logo.
Interaction: Wouter routes, accessible dialogs, event filtering, calendar export, gallery lightbox, sermon reading. Public Malagasy interface. Photos labeled illustrations, dates/content examples labeled. Never invent actual people or contact details.
Admin: same green palette, compact tables and working forms. Google/email login on a tabbed page, no automatic administrator grants. Protect membership data server-side with explicit role table.
